# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors and reviewers are expected to keep this project respectful,
constructive, and focused on learning. This is an academic project, so feedback
should help the maintainer understand both what to improve and why it matters.

## Expected Behaviour

- Use welcoming and professional language.
- Give actionable feedback with examples where possible.
- Respect the coursework context and academic integrity requirements.
- Keep discussions focused on the project, its documentation, and its code.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, insults, intimidation, or discriminatory remarks.
- Sharing private information without consent.
- Encouraging plagiarism, misrepresentation, or academic misconduct.
- Deliberately disrupting review, testing, or project maintenance.

## Enforcement

For coursework review, concerns should be escalated through the appropriate
Singapore Polytechnic module or school channel. For local project maintenance,
the maintainer may remove inappropriate content and decline contributions that
violate this code.
